Subliminal Alignment Distillation Prevents Emergent Misalignment

A lightweight distillation idea for improving alignment while training on largely unrelated tasks: science, math, coding, and other capability data can carry an alignment signal through context.

Atharv Naphade* Alignment Distillation In-Context Learning

Emergent misalignment can appear even when training does not obviously target harmful behavior. This project asks whether the distillation process itself can quietly carry a countervailing alignment signal.

The method introduces Subliminal Alignment Distillation: a drop-in variant of on-policy distillation that places a constitutional system prompt around the teacher signal. The key claim is not that unrelated tasks magically become safety data, but that the training context can shape how the student internalizes behavior while still learning useful capabilities.

Core idea: preserve the capability benefits of distillation, while making the latent policy update less likely to amplify misaligned patterns.

The Setup

Standard on-policy distillation asks a student model to imitate outputs produced during training. Subliminal Alignment Distillation changes the context that generates those outputs: the teacher is conditioned by a constitutional instruction, and the student learns from the resulting behavior across unrelated domains.

Why It Matters

Alignment methods often compete with capability training for data, compute, and simplicity. A drop-in distillation approach is attractive because it can be inserted into existing training workflows without requiring every example to be an explicit safety demonstration.

If alignment-relevant behavior can transfer through context during ordinary capability training, then post-training can become both cheaper and harder to accidentally misdirect.
